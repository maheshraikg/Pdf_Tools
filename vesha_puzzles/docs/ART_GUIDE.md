# Art guide — Vesha Puzzles, first release

Everything in the app that is drawn is a **placeholder** generated by
`tool/gen_placeholders.dart`. Real art replaces those files **in place,
with the same file name and the same aspect ratio** — no code changes
needed. After replacing files, set `"placeholder": false` and fill in
`"credits"` in each `pack.json` / `dressup.json`.

General rules

- sRGB colour, no embedded text or watermarks (the app adds titles).
- Respectful depiction: no deity faces/idols as the main subject (the
  temple car can be shown from outside, idol hidden or veiled), no mockery
  of ritual forms. When in doubt, ask the reviewer listed in
  `docs/CONTENT_TO_REVIEW.md`.
- Deliver a layered master (PSD/KRA/SVG) **and** the export below.

Specs (each): **2048 × 1536 px (4:3 landscape)**, JPEG quality 85–90,
≤ 900 KB. The puzzle is cut into up to 80 pieces, so:

- keep detail and texture across the **whole** picture; large flat areas
  (plain sky, plain walls) make pieces impossible to place;
- keep faces and the main subject inside the central 85 %;
- avoid thin repeating stripes (moiré when scaled down);
- the top-left 20 % × bottom 12 % may be covered by the title on the
  share image — keep it free of key detail.

(The pack cover is the first puzzle image; no separate cover is needed.)

## 2. Dress-up layers — 1 base + 19 option layers

All files are **1024 × 1536 px transparent PNG on the same canvas**, drawn
over the same base figure so they line up when stacked (no offsets in
code). Reference points: face centre (512, 500); shoulders y ≈ 720;
waist y ≈ 1120; feet y ≈ 1500. Child-friendly, front view, arms slightly
out. Draw order in the app (bottom → top): base, face, costume, chest,
shoulders, ears, headgear, prop.

## 3. Vesha, the guide — 3 images

`assets/guide/vesha_idle.png`, `vesha_happy.png`, `vesha_think.png` —
**768 × 768 px transparent PNG**, a child performer in a small kireeta,

## 4. App icon

- Master **1024 × 1024 px** PNG (no transparency, no text).
- Exports: `android/app/src/main/res/mipmap-{mdpi,hdpi,xhdpi,xxhdpi,xxxhdpi}/ic_launcher.png`
  at 48, 72, 96, 144, 192 px, and `docs/icon_512.png` (Play Store).
- Optional adaptive icon: 432 × 432 foreground (safe zone 288 px circle)
  + flat background colour.

## 5. Optional

- Play Store feature graphic 1024 × 500 JPEG/PNG.
- Sounds (`assets/audio/*.wav`, keep the names): pick, tap, snap, place
  (< 0.4 s), unlock (< 1 s), complete (< 3 s), music_loop (seamless loop,
  20–60 s, mono 22–44 kHz). Chende/maddale/tala recordings would be ideal
  — only with the musicians' written permission.
