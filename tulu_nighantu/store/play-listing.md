## Full description

ತುಳು ನಿಘಂಟು – Tulu Nighantu is a free, offline dictionary for Tulu, with
Kannada and English meanings, and a friendly way to learn the Tulu script
(Tulu lipi / Tigalari).

📖 Dictionary
• Search in Tulu, Kannada or English – or in English letters (e.g. "neer")
• Every word shown in Kannada script, Tulu lipi and romanised Tulu
• Categories: family, body, food, nature, animals, numbers, time and more
• Word of the day and favourites
• Add your own words when one is missing

🔤 Translate
• Type Kannada or English and get Tulu, word by word
• Listen, copy, or share the result as a picture

✍️ Learn Tulu lipi
• The whole alphabet with barakhadi (ಕಾಗುಣಿತ)
• Watch each letter being written, then trace it with your finger
• Earn stars as you practise

🔁 Script converter
• Turn Kannada text into Tulu lipi instantly

✨ Ask AI (optional, needs internet)
• Get a suggested Tulu translation for words and sentences that are not in
  the dictionary. AI answers are marked "not verified".

🔊 Pronunciation with your phone's Kannada voice.

Works without internet. No ads, no sign-in.
Tulu lipi font: Mallige (SIL Open Font License).
