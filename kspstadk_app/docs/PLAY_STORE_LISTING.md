## English (en-IN)

**Short description (80):**
LBA question banks, lesson-wise PDFs and orders for Karnataka teachers

**Full description:**
KSPSTADK is the Kannada-first hub of study materials and updates for Karnataka teachers, now as a fast native app.

✅ Classes 1–10: browse by class → subject → medium
✅ LBA (Learning-outcome Based Assessment) question banks: lesson-wise question and answer PDFs
✅ Open, download and share PDFs with one tap, in a built-in PDF reader (night mode, jump to page)
✅ My Downloads: read saved PDFs without internet
✅ Department orders, circulars and latest education news
✅ FA/SA question papers, Nali-Kali, Kalika Chetarike, teacher articles, quizzes
✅ New-post notifications, only for the topics and classes you choose
✅ Bookmarks, search, dark mode, Kannada / English
✅ No ads, no login

Note: this app is not affiliated with any government body. All content comes from kspstadk.com.
